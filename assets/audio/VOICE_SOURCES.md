## First Mandarin battle-voice batch

- Source: `R29-39-Chinese Screams in Battle.wav`, uploaded by Craig Smith on Freesound.
- Source page: <https://freesound.org/people/craigsmith/sounds/479582/>
- License shown on the source page: Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
- Source description identifies the recording as human Mandarin battle voices from a vintage film sound-effects collection.
- Local derivatives: `wusheng.mp3`, `longdan.mp3`, `qixi.mp3`, `kurou.mp3`, `ganglie.mp3`, `quhu.mp3`, `luanwu.mp3`, `lieren.mp3`, `duanbing.mp3`, and `qiangxi.mp3`.

The derivatives are short excerpts with only trimming, fades, frequency cleanup, mono conversion, and loudness normalization. No speech synthesis, pitch shifting, or voice conversion was used.
